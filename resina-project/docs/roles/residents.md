# Resident / User Guide

This guide is for residents using the RESINA mobile app.

## Quick Start

1. Open the app and sign in with your email and password.
2. If you are new, tap Register Now and complete your profile details.
3. Use the bottom navigation to move between Home, News, History, and Profile.
4. If you forget your password, tap Forgot Password and follow the reset link sent to your email.

## Common Tasks

| Action | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Click | Open Home to check the current tide level, alert status, next high tide, and next low tide. |
| Navigate | Move to Weather to review the latest temperature, humidity, wind speed, and advisory. |
| Navigate | Open Tide to view the Tide Monitor, tide trend, and hourly tide estimates. |
| Navigate | Open News to browse official announcements, filter by All, Warning, or Emergency, and search posts. |
| Tap | Open an announcement to read details, view attached images, and add or review comments. |
| Navigate | Open History to review past water-level records, filter by status, and load older entries. |
| Edit | Open Profile to update your avatar, name, phone number, address / purok, or password. |
| Submit | Sign out when you finish using the app or when you share the device with someone else. |

## Troubleshooting

- If you cannot sign in, confirm that your email and password are correct and that your account email is verified.
- If announcements, tide data, or history look stale, check your connection and refresh the app.
- If the password reset message does not arrive, check spam or junk mail and request a new link.
- If your profile changes do not save, make sure the phone number and address fields are complete and valid.

## Data Privacy & Ethics

- Your profile contains personal data such as your name, phone number, resident status, and address / purok. Keep that information accurate and private.
- The phone number in your profile is used for urgent SMS alerts. Use only your own number and do not share it casually.
- Treat any passwords, reset links, access tokens, and encrypted account data as confidential. Never forward them to other people.
- Use comments responsibly. Share only information that is relevant to barangay updates, safety, and community notices.
- Do not post other people’s personal details, unverified rumors, or sensitive incident information in comments or messages.
